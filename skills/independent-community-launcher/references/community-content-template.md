## Funnel

```text
Public content → explanation/Podcast → opt-in email or LINE → small experiment/event → core community → paid event, project, or membership
```

## 7-day experiment

1. Define a skill or asset.
2. Ask AI for possible offers.
3. Choose a target person.
4. Write a small offer.
5. Contact three people with consent and no spam.
6. Record time, cost, reaction, and result.
7. Share both the outcome and the failure.

## KPI

Measure profile visits, saves, shares, opt-ins, event registrations, attendance, repeat participation, qualified inquiries, cost per acquired member, 30-day retention, and revenue. Do not use follower count or views as the only success metric.

## Monetization disclosure

State whether a post or episode includes referral compensation, sponsorship, affiliate revenue, gifted products, paid placement, or a platform campaign. Verify current terms and eligibility immediately before publication.
