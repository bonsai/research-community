# Ecosystem Governance Reference

`buraiha-p` is the **top-level producer agent and overall strategy owner** for the ecosystem.

`research-community` is a research, brand, community-asset, and learning workspace under P's direction. It supplies evidence, reusable content, platform/API research, audience insights, and retrospectives. It does not own the overall portfolio, final priorities, experiment approvals, or stop/repeat decisions.

## Repository hierarchy

```text
buraiha-p  [overall strategy, portfolio, priorities, producer decisions]
├── research-community     [research, brand, community assets]
├── community-based-sales  [content, relationship, product/sales execution]
└── MX                      [marketing model, hypotheses, measurement]
```

## Decision flow

```text
P sets strategic priority
  → research-community gathers evidence and assets
  → MX creates market hypothesis and measurement plan
  → P approves experiment, owner, budget, and stop condition
  → community-based-sales executes
  → feedback and results return to MX
  → P decides repeat, revise, partner, pause, or stop
```

## Scope of this repository

- Maintain brand and community materials.
- Maintain platform/API research and source links.
- Record audience, culture, and field observations.
- Supply reusable podcast, social, event, and community assets.
- Record evidence and learning requested by P.

Do not store API keys, payment credentials, private member records, customer addresses, or private chat transcripts here. Do not make income guarantees. Label advertising, referrals, sponsorships, gifted products, and affiliate compensation.

For the complete operating model, read [buraiha-p's ecosystem governance specification](https://github.com/bonsai/buraiha-p/blob/main/docs/ecosystem-governance.md).
